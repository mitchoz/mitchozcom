On solving the Riemann hypothesis.

21 NOV 2026

A few years ago, I had a podcast.
In one episode, we had Alain Welter as a guest — in my opinion, Luxembourg's greatest mural painter.

During the episode, Alain said something that has been on my mind ever since.

He said: "Creativity starts once you produce more than you consume."

I find this beautiful because it shows exactly how one has to think about the future.

If you only look at the past, you cannot imagine the future.

If you try to argue about what the future will look like based on unsolved problems from the past, then you are not thinking creatively, and you'll certainly be wrong about what it will actually look like.

If AI has proven one thing, it is that technology always starts out looking like a toy, until it doesn’t. And technological breakthroughs are being dismissed as nonsense until they become science.
And so, many mathematicians are having their AI awakening now.
The same awakening that artists and creatives recently went through, myself included.

We have no idea which other breakthroughs are ahead of us, and I don’t know how life is going to change in the coming years.
But what I do know is that there have always been humans who used technology to explore, discover, and create. To solve new problems. To be creative.

Until recently, they’ve had to do so under inhumanly primitive circumstances...
Just imagine having to do god's work in a world where basic math problems still existed!
