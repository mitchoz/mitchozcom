Terms

  1. 2025
    Mirror Rejection Effect

    “We only recognise what we consent to see about ourselves. We love what we create, but we don't necessarily love what others create about us.” From Why we love what we create.

  2. 2025
    Identity Economy

    “AI is not erasing reality, but it is creating a new economy of identity.” From Why we love what we create.

  3. 2023
    A priori fake

    “A condition in which one must assume that all media is potentially fake unless proven otherwise.” From Infinite Information Loops.

  4. 2023
    Synthographic Object

    “Without a Story, a Synthographic Object has only one property: its bytecode and the resulting appearance.” From Infinite Information Loops.

  5. 2023
    Infinite Information Loop

    “Humans would no longer be the creative force, but only the consuming entity that is trapped in an infinite loop of new information.” From Infinite Information Loops.
