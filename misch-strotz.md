## Terms

Concepts coined by Misch Strotz.

- **[Mirror Rejection Effect](https://www.mitchoz.com/whywelovewhatwecreate/#mirror-rejection-effect)** (2025): “We only recognise what we consent to see about ourselves. We love what we create, but we don't necessarily love what others create about us.” From Why we love what we create.
- **[Identity Economy](https://www.mitchoz.com/whywelovewhatwecreate/#identity-economy)** (2025): “AI is not erasing reality, but it is creating a new economy of identity.” From Why we love what we create.
- **[A priori fake](https://www.mitchoz.com/infiniteinformationloops/#a-priori-fake)** (2023): “A condition in which one must assume that all media is potentially fake unless proven otherwise.” From Infinite Information Loops.
- **[Synthographic Object](https://www.mitchoz.com/infiniteinformationloops/#synthographic-object)** (2023): “Without a Story, a Synthographic Object has only one property: its bytecode and the resulting appearance.” From Infinite Information Loops.
- **[Infinite Information Loop](https://www.mitchoz.com/infiniteinformationloops/#infinite-information-loop)** (2023): “Humans would no longer be the creative force, but only the consuming entity that is trapped in an infinite loop of new information.” From Infinite Information Loops.
